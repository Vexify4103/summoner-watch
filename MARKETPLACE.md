# Marketplace submission draft — 0.1.7

## Product description

Summoner Watch brings manual enemy summoner spell timers for League of Legends to your Stream Deck. Press when an enemy uses a spell, watch its cooldown, and hear a sound when it returns. Champion icons, spells, boots and levels load from the game's local data.

Tap an enemy key for the first spell, double-tap for the second, or hold for a top lane quest Teleport where available. Choose spell order and ignored spells. Use dials or Timer keys to adjust or cancel countdowns. Undo steps back through timer starts.

Requires Windows 10 or newer and Stream Deck software 6.9 or newer. Automatic Cosmic Insight detection requires your own Riot API key; manual toggling works without one. Spell use is never detected automatically.

AI tools assisted with development, text and gallery composition. The product logo was AI-generated. Gallery images use official Elgato hardware photos and captured plugin output.
